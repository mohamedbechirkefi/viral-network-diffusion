## Checklist

- [x] **Evidence paths exist.** Every row's evidence path exists (script check, 0 missing).
- [x] **Numerical claims are mapped.** Every numerical claim maps to an experiment id, run id, config hash and figure or table. Synthesis rows cite the result rows they rest on.
- [x] **External claims are mapped.** Every external claim maps to a source actually opened, and its verification level is in the `status` column.
  - Abstract-only sources are marked "abstract only".
  - Metadata-only sources appear only in EXT-08, as attributions, and no statement rests on their content.
- [x] **Numbers are audited against raw outputs** (denominators, units, precision, uncertainty and sample counts).
  - The frozen analysis was recomputed from all eight raw results: 177 checks, 0 failures.
  - Every number in the manuscript, the supplementary file and the five reports is traced (`number_trace.csv`).
  - All 370 printed estimate-and-interval triples match one committed row jointly.
- [x] **Claims boundary.** No claim concerns real platforms or guaranteed virality. Searching the register for "facebook", "platform" or "guarantee" finds only EXT-09, the data provenance of the real network.
- [x] **Simulated claims are labelled.** Every result row's text begins "Simulation" or "EXPLORATORY simulation", and the scope column says "simulation".
- [x] **Second review pass.** It challenged the preferred hypothesis (`research/publication/review_pass_2.md`): 8 challenges held in full or in part, and the manuscript was revised (version 0.2).

## Claims removed or qualified at M4

- **Threshold expression.** The reports had attributed `p_c = <k>/(<k^2> - <k>)` to Newman et al. (2001) and Pastor-Satorras et al. (2015). The expression was not found in their abstracts, so it is now a definition and the citations are qualified (EXP-002 and EXP-004 reports).
- **IC definition.** The reports said the IC definition "follows" Kempe et al. (2003). That abstract does not name the model, so the claim is qualified (EXP-001 report).
- **LT background.** The EXP-003 report cited Granovetter (1978) and Centola and Macy (2007) as support for the LT background. Both are metadata only, so they are now attributions.
- **Draft manuscript.** The draft's threshold-location mechanism (title and abstract), the WS "clustered ring delays the outbreak" mechanism and the general modelling lesson were removed or narrowed by the second review pass.
