## 2. Research question and operationalisation

**Question (section 10).** How do network topology and initial exposure influence diffusion independently of content quality?

**Operationalisation.**
- *Content quality* is not modelled as a content attribute. It is held fixed by giving every edge the same transmission probability `p` (IC) or every node the same fractional threshold `phi` (LT). Any difference between topologies at matched `p` or `phi` is therefore a structural effect, not a content effect, within the model.
- *Topology* has three levels with matched node count `N` and target mean degree `<k>`: Erdos-Renyi G(n,M) (baseline), Watts-Strogatz small world, Barabasi-Albert scale free.
- *Initial exposure* has two factors: seed selection rule (uniform random node; highest degree) and seed count (1 node; 1 percent of nodes).

## 3. Hypotheses (primary, EXP-002)

- **H0.** At matched `N`, `<k>`, transmission probability and seeding (one uniformly random seed), the expected final cascade fraction averaged over the frozen `p` grid does not differ between a Watts-Strogatz graph and an Erdos-Renyi graph, nor between a Barabasi-Albert graph and an Erdos-Renyi graph: `Delta_WS = 0` and `Delta_BA = 0`.
- **H1 (nondirectional).** At least one of `Delta_WS`, `Delta_BA` differs from 0.
- **Contradiction criterion.** H1 is not supported for a contrast if its Holm-adjusted 95 percent bootstrap interval contains 0. A non-rejection is not evidence of equivalence: equivalence is claimed only if the interval lies entirely inside the smallest effect size of interest, `[-0.02, +0.02]` in cascade fraction (planning value, to be confirmed at the freeze).
- Directional expectations from the literature (recorded before any run, not tested as the primary hypothesis): degree heterogeneity lowers the IC percolation threshold (Pastor-Satorras and Vespignani 2001; Newman, Strogatz and Watts 2001), so BA is expected to exceed ER at small `p`; at large `p` the ordering may reverse. This possible crossing is why the primary contrast is nondirectional.

## 4. Baseline, metrics and estimand

- **Baseline (primary comparator).** Erdos-Renyi G(n,M) graph, IC diffusion, one uniformly random seed. It is the simplest graph with the target size and mean degree and no imposed structure.
- **Primary metric.** Final cascade fraction `S = |A_final| / N`, where `A_final` is the set of nodes ever activated. Unit: fraction of nodes in [0, 1]. Direction: none imposed (nondirectional H1). Justification: it is the outcome most directly tied to "how far diffusion goes", it is defined for every model and topology, and it is bounded, which keeps the bootstrap well behaved.
- **Primary estimand.** For topology `T` in {WS, BA}: `Delta_T = E_r[ Sbar_{T,r} - Sbar_{ER,r} ]`, where `Sbar_{T,r}` is the mean of `S` over all cascades and all `p` in the frozen grid on replicate `r` of topology `T`. Replicates of different topologies sharing index `r` form a matched set (same `N`, `<k>`, master seed derivation and seeded node indices).
- **Secondary metrics.**
  - Depth `D`: maximum generation in the activation tree (seed = generation 0).
  - Duration `T`: number of synchronous rounds with at least one new activation. In synchronous IC, `D = T` by construction; this equality is a test invariant, and both are reported separately only for LT, where they can differ.
  - Time to half `T50`: first round at which half of the final active set is reached.
  - Inequality `G`: Gini coefficient of the final sizes of the cascades on one graph at one setting (how unequal outcomes are for identical content).
  - Large-cascade probability: share of cascades with `S >= 0.10` (planning threshold).
  - Variance decomposition: share of variance of `S` between topologies, between graph replicates within topology, and between cascades within graph (distinguishes stochastic variability from topology effects).
  - Structural virality (Goel et al. 2016): mean pairwise distance in the activation tree, exploratory secondary.

## 5. Design

- **Experimental unit.** The graph replicate `r` (one generated graph). Cascades on the same graph are correlated and are not independent units.
- **Graph generation.** `N = 1000`, target `<k> = 6` (planning values).
  - ER: `G(n, M)` with `M = N <k> / 2 = 3000` edges.
  - WS: ring lattice with `k = 6`, rewiring probability `beta = 0.1` (planning value), `N k / 2 = 3000` edges.
  - BA: `m = 3`; the networkx generator gives `m (N - m) = 2991` edges, mean degree 5.982.
  - Matching tolerance: realised mean degree within 1 percent of the target; recorded per graph. Sensitivity S3 removes the residual mismatch.
- **Seeds.** One master seed per experiment. Per-run seeds derived deterministically with `numpy.random.SeedSequence(master).spawn`, keyed by (topology, replicate, setting). Matched sets: the same replicate index `r` uses the same seed-node draw stream across topologies (common random numbers where node indices are comparable).
- **Replication.** `R = 30` graph replicates per topology (at least 5 required; 30 chosen for interval precision), `C = 200` cascades per (graph, setting).
- **Sample-size rationale.** The interval width depends on the between-replicate standard deviation of `Sbar`. Planning target: 95 percent interval half-width at most 0.01 for each primary contrast. With `R` paired replicates, half-width is about `1.96 sd_diff / sqrt(R)`; `R = 30` meets the target if `sd_diff <= 0.028`. The EXP-001 smoke run (exploratory) estimates `sd_diff`; if it is larger, `R` is raised before the freeze. `R` is never changed after confirmatory results are seen.
- **Uncertainty.** Paired cluster bootstrap over replicate index `r` (resample matched sets with replacement), `B = 10000`, percentile intervals. Holm adjustment over the two primary contrasts (familywise 0.05). Secondary metrics: unadjusted 95 percent intervals, labelled as such. Raw per-cascade and per-replicate distributions are exported and plotted.
- **Exclusion rules.** No cascade is excluded. A graph that fails generation (for example a connected WS variant exceeding its retry budget) is regenerated with the next derived seed; every failure is counted and reported. Isolated nodes and disconnected components are kept as part of the topology; the giant-component fraction is recorded per graph. A random seed may fall on an isolated node; such cascades have `S = 1/N` and are kept.
- **Stopping rule.** Fixed matrix, no optional stopping, no additional replicates after results are seen. If the compute budget (4 hours, planning value) is exceeded, the run stops, the completed part is reported as incomplete, and no partial conclusion is drawn on missing cells.

## 7. Sensitivity analyses (planned before any result)

- **S1, threshold rescaling (could invalidate the main interpretation).** Repeat the comparison at matched `p / p_c(G)`, where `p_c(G) = <k> / (<k^2> - <k>)` is the bond-percolation threshold estimate of each graph. If the topology differences vanish at matched `p / p_c`, the main result is explained by the degree moments alone (a known mechanism), and "topology effect" must be reported as "degree-heterogeneity effect".
- **S2, degree-preserving null.** Compare BA graphs with degree-preserving randomisations of the same graphs (double-edge swaps). If they do not differ, the BA effect is carried by the degree sequence, not by other structure.
- **S3, exact matching.** Remove the residual edge-count mismatch (trim ER and WS to the BA edge count) and repeat the primary contrasts.
- **S4, size.** `N` in {500, 2000} at fixed `<k>` to check finite-size dependence (optional if the budget is threatened).

## 9. Confounders and threats considered in the design

- Mean degree and edge count (matched within 1 percent; S3).
- Degree heterogeneity versus other structure (S1, S2).
- Clustering in WS depends on `beta`; one `beta` value is a limitation, not a general WS claim.
- Node labels are meaningful in BA (early nodes are hubs) and WS (ring order); matched seed-node indices are therefore not exchangeable across topologies, which is why random seeding draws node indices uniformly and the highest-degree rule is defined by degree, not index.
- Finite size (S4).
